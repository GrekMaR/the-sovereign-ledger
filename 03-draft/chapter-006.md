# Chapter 006: Raising the Banner

## Hook
The guild is invited to bid on an entire district of guild-licensed warehouses, a move that would turn an effective trading network into a sovereign infrastructure base.

## Synopsis
Lys prepares the bid using a hybrid strategy: he shortens the cartel's supply chain by buying up side contracts, creates a fake liquidity crisis in the district, and then triggers a public panic in the bidding pool. The guild wins the auction by absorbing the risk others refuse to carry. The district is not merely land; it is a proof of legitimacy and a platform for holding labor, storage, and magical production in one hand. By the chapter's end, a new stage of the guild's rise begins: it is no longer a client of the market but a node capable of shaping it.

## Progression milestone
The guild gains territory and expands its political and economic reach.

## Stakes
The victory increases the guild's obligations, enemies, and exposure, making every future decision more consequential.

## Arc
Act II — building a monopolistic engine

## Goal
Show the protagonist out-bidding enemies not through brute force but through economic shaping and strategic pressure.
