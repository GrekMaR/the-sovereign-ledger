# Chapter 004: The Lantern Market

## Hook
A forgotten trading district called the Lantern Market opens briefly under a celestial weather anomaly, allowing the guild to buy cheap and sell precious goods before the city's elite discover the gap.

## Synopsis
Lys turns knowledge into execution, building a temporary network of brokers, warehouse hands, and shrine-linked transport routes that can move goods at a speed the nobles cannot match. The chapter centres on his understanding that value is not static: a resource is cheap at one point of the chain and priceless at another. By controlling the middle of the chain, the guild steals margin from the cartel. His first small monopoly begins to take shape not in steel or steelclad armies, but in timing, access, and logistics.

## Progression milestone
The guild gains its first genuine monopoly corridor and proves that the modern toolkit of arbitrage can create wealth from thin air.

## Stakes
The cartel responds with violence, legal filings, and attempts to buy out the guild's partners.

## Arc
Act II — building a monopolistic engine

## Goal
Convert early success into durable market power.
