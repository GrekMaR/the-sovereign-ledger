# Chapter 007: The Cartel Break

## Hook
A noble cartel tries to corner the guild's trade route by a coordinated export refusal and a fake debt scandal, but Lys sees the panic for what it is: a short-sell strategy disguised as market discipline.

## Synopsis
Lys weaponises the cartel's own methods against them, creating an information asymmetry that lets the guild sell short its rivals' credit and buy time to restructure its own contracts. He leaks a series of carefully fabricated but plausible supply shortages to the market, causing the cartel's hold to buckle under the pressure of panic. The chapter is a lesson in game theory: the guild survives not by retaliation but by controlling expectations. By the end, the cartel's alliance fractures, and the guild proves it can manipulate an entire market without depending on a battlefield victory.

## Progression milestone
The guild turns from a target into a market-maker, achieving a decisive strategic win against the old order.

## Stakes
The cartel won't simply retreat; it will try to destroy the guild's legitimacy and hold the city against it.

## Arc
Act III — empire-building and cartel war

## Goal
Demonstrate the guild's mastery of strategic manipulation and information warfare.
