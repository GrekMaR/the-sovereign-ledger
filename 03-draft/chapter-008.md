# Chapter 008: The Blessing License

## Hook
The gods open a celestial blessing auction for a critical trade license, and every elite house is already prepared to bid far beyond any ordinary guild's means.

## Synopsis
Lys discovers the true economics of divine licensing: the auction rewards legitimacy, hidden covenants, and the ability to turn a blessing into productive infrastructure. He leverages the guild's district, creditor structure, and market share to secure a financing package larger than the noble houses expect from a common guild. In the final bid, he refuses to chase the gods' vanity and instead anchors the blessing to a solemn city-wide supply network, forcing the divine auctioneer to accept a value proposition beyond simple prestige. The guild wins a license that transforms it from rich to powerful.

## Progression milestone
The guild gains a blessing license that enlarges its reach, prestige, and authority.

## Stakes
A blessing license means a city, a god, and an entire network of expectations now watch the guild closely.

## Arc
Act III — empire-building and cartel war

## Goal
Turn economic leverage into a divine, political, and territorial advantage.
