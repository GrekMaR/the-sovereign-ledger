# Chapter 009: The Sovereign Ledger

## Hook
With the guild's resources, contracts, and prestige now at scale, a critical dungeon corridor comes up for charter, and the guild's strongest rivals prepare to fence it away from any newcomer.

## Synopsis
Lys leads the guild into a final, brutal contest over monopoly rights. He does not rely on brute force; instead, he engineers a combined bid using the guild's own trade routes, blessing revenue, and market-control logic. The confrontation reveals the core truth of the story: power in this world is not merely military or magical; it is the capability to command access, build trust, and absorb risk. The guild wins the corridor and thereby secures a strategic resource choke point that will let it command a larger section of the sky-city economy.

## Progression milestone
The guild reaches the threshold of sovereign status, holding a critical resource chokepoint and a legal claim that rivals cannot dismiss.

## Stakes
The victory cements the guild's power, but now the guild must decide how to use it without becoming the very cartel it destroyed.

## Arc
Act III — empire-building and cartel war

## Goal
Achieve a strategic monopoly that positions the guild as a true empire-tier power.
