# Chapter 002: The First Charter

## Hook
A divine auctioneer opens a sealed bid for a low-tier trade charter, and the guild must compete against old-money noble houses that never expected a bankrupt underdog to show up.

## Synopsis
Lys studies the auction rules, realises the gods prefer confidence and predictable volume over pure wealth, and formulates a bid built on hidden reserves and strategic underpricing. He pairs a wheat surplus with a transport caravan and a promise of tax remittance, weaponising arbitrage to undercut the cartel's price and win a charter nobody believed could be taken. The guild gains legal standing, a market corridor, and a reason for the city to look at them twice.

## Progression milestone
The guild secures its first charter, establishing revenue and operational legitimacy.

## Stakes
The charter unlocks access to the trade lane and the possibility of future growth, but the cartel sees a threat now and will retaliate.

## Arc
Act I — survival and first leverage

## Goal
Turn a desperate bid into a legitimate commercial advantage.
