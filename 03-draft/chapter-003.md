# Chapter 003: The Price of Silence

## Hook
The noble cartel tests the guild's nerve by arranging a theft of its reserve stock and a whisper campaign that labels Lys a liar and a fraud.

## Synopsis
Lys learns that the true enemy is not physical force but reputational damage. He refuses to pay the cartel's demand for silence and instead purchases the silence of his own doubts, using a small reserve of capital and a public settlement that humbles but does not crush the nobles. The guild survives because it refuses to be cornered into a panic sale. A rogue logistics courier reveals a deeper possibility: the cartel's hold on a critical dungeon resource route is weaker than it pretends.

## Progression milestone
Lys proves he can turn social warfare into strategic advantage and begins to see cartel weakness in the hidden margins.

## Stakes
The guild survives, but the cartel learns who to target next and begins to weaponise debt and rumor.

## Arc
Act I — survival and first leverage

## Goal
Show that the protagonist can fight on economic and informational terrain, not just force.
