# Chapter 010: A Realm in Brass

## Hook
The guild stands at the threshold of becoming an empire, but in a world of gods, contracts, and nobles, the hardest part is not taking power — it is deciding what kind of power it will keep.

## Synopsis
Lys and the guild hold the district, the blessing licenses, the trade corridor, and the loyalty of a growing network of brokers, carriers, and guild officers. Yet the final challenge is not external; it is internal. He must choose between becoming a new cartel built on the same logic as the old houses, or building a guild empire that values scale without exploitation. The chapter ends with the guild crowned as a new sovereign power, but not as a glorious conquest; as a disciplined machine that can survive the gods, the nobles, and the market itself. The last beat resolves in a broadened horizon: this is not just the rise of a guild, but the beginning of a kingdom built on economics, logistics, and will.

## Progression milestone
The guild transforms into a sovereign trading empire and proves that strategic economic power can rival divine and noble authority.

## Stakes
The final decision frames the future of the empire and whether the protagonist will build something enduring or merely replace one cartel with another.

## Arc
Act III — empire-building and cartel war

## Goal
Close the arc with a clear rise to sovereignty and a set-up for what a guild empire means in this world.
