# Chapter 001: The Bankrupt Guild

## Hook
When Lys Vey enters the Hall of Ledgered Debts, the guild he inherited is already on the brink of seizure by a noble cartel, and the only asset left worth anything is a single, desperate idea: make the guild valuable before it is taken apart.

## Synopsis
Lys learns the guild owes more than it can ever pay and that the strongest rivals are not swordsmen but creditors, tax collectors, and gods who view commerce as a battlefield. In a desperate bid, he identifies a hidden choke point in the guild's logistics route: a failing granary connected to a relic trade lane. By engineering a legal quirk and a public debt restructuring, he turns a sure collapse into a trial of survival and begins to prove that the guild can become a strategic player.

## Progression milestone
Lys gains his first actionable leverage by converting a bankrupt liability into a market opportunity; he takes the first step toward a commercial empire.

## Stakes
If he fails, the guild is stripped and dissolved. If he succeeds, he secures a future for the guild and a reputation that can attract capital.

## Arc
Act I — survival and first leverage

## Goal
Establish the opening threat, the guild's weakness, and the first strategic win.
