# Chapter 005: A Debt with Teeth

## Hook
A divine lender offers a staggering line of credit to the guild, but the terms are as dangerous as they are attractive, and the offer comes wrapped in a public challenge.

## Synopsis
Lys cannot outgrow debt without accepting debt. He uses venture-capital logic to structure the loan as leverage for expansion rather than surrender. He offers the lender a share of future guild revenue, condenses the guild's obligations, and establishes a more efficient capital structure. The credit lets the guild expand its warehouses and buy a stake in a dungeon resource concession, but the loan slowly begins to shape the guild's choices. He learns that the gods do not value generosity; they value clarity, strategic certainty, and control.

## Progression milestone
The guild gains capital, but the cost is exposed leverage and a new covenant that binds the guild to its ambitions.

## Stakes
One wrong move could let the creditors seize the guild, but any delay leaves the cartel untouchable.

## Arc
Act II — building a monopolistic engine

## Goal
Show the protagonist mastering risk, leverage, and venture capital in a fantasy setting.
