# Magic System

## Core principle

The world runs on a blend of divine favor, ritualized essence, and measurable energetic flows. Magic is not abstract and freeform; it is operationally similar to a logistics and finance system in which efficiency, control, and risk management determine mastery.

## Systems and practices

- Contracted Blessings: divine permissions that permit access to resources, legal privileges, or tactical boons.
- Essence Routing: channeling stored magical energy into infrastructure, production, and battlefield advantage.
- Writs and seals: enchanted legal instruments that bind merchants, guilds, and land rights to specific claims.
- Guild-linked sigils: magical infrastructure that allows a guild to coordinate operations across territory, warehouses, and dungeons.

## Costs and consequences

- Overinvestment in any single resource flow creates instability and debt penalties.
- Unstable magical contracts create spiritual backlash, a collapse in reputation, or divine retaliation.
- The protagonist can use logistical thinking to create leverage, but not without paying the hidden cost of concentration and risk.
- Magical power cannot fully replace political legitimacy; a guild that cannot hold the field economically will lose its edge.
