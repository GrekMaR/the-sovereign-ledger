# Magic System

- **Grace** (unit: gleams) is faith and kept promises made measurable. Temples convert coin to grace at ~120 crowns/gleam, never the reverse. Gods feed on sustained grace flow, not lump sums.
- **Auctions:** open ascending in grace; Sael's Hammer falls after three heartbeats of silence; 20-gleam bid bond; bidders may offer structured terms under old auction law.
- **Blessings/licenses** (e.g. Ilvenne's Rain-Tithe, Sael's Weigher's License) grant economic power in exchange for tithe upkeep; breach of tithe forfeits them.
- **Korrath's Vault of Vows** lends against oaths. Oathbrand: a mark that burns when a covenant is at risk.
- **Ledger-sight** (Guildmaster's Bind): contracts under divine witness appear as threads — gold kept, grey pending, red-black breaking. Cost: headache, nosebleed, blackout if held. Tier I touch only; Tier II whole room; Tier III reads strain (default risk); Tier IV brief city-wide glimpse.
- **Maruth** sells dungeon lots only at the Deep Hammer to bidders who have walked the Deepway.
