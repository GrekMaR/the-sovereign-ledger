# Truth Lock

Authoritative detail lives in `02-outline/series-bible.md`. These facts are immutable for Book One.

- Vessant is a river-delta trade city beneath the Firmament Exchange, where the Scaled Pantheon (Sael, Ilvenne, Korrath, Maruth, Oda) auctions rain, roads, districts, dungeon concessions, and blessing licenses for **grace**.
- Only the 40 Seat-holding chartered guilds may bid. Seats cannot be sold; the Guild Court awards a foreclosed guild's Seat to its chartered creditor.
- A charter won at the Exchange stays mortal writs against the winner for 90 days.
- Lys Vey (24) is secretly Theo Marsh, a London turnaround/distressed-debt consultant who woke in Lys's body four days before Chapter 1. No character learns this in Book One.
- The Cinderwick Company (Tallow Street, Seat 39) opens Book One owing House Dain 41,000 crowns plus 6,200 to small creditors, with 212 crowns cash and 9 gleams of grace.
- The Gilded Concord (Dain, Oskel, Merrow, Vantheil, Castellane, Ruske, Hale) is the noble cartel antagonist; Ser Aurel Dain is its Speaker.
- The guildmaster is bound to the Charter Stone; foreclosure means Unbinding, which usually kills.
