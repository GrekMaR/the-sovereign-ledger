# Truth Lock

## Immutable facts

- The setting is a celestial marketocracy where deities auction privileges, land charters, blessing licenses, and resource monopolies.
- Guilds compete not only for war rights but also for economic access, logistical dominance, and social legitimacy.
- The protagonist begins with a bankrupt underdog guild with little standing, no capital, and a reputation for failure.
- The strongest power structures are not just noble houses but cartel-like guild coalitions and divine trade syndicates.
- Modern knowledge can materially change outcomes when translated into a world that still runs on faith, ritual, and material scarcity.

## Story contract

- The guild is the protagonist's primary vehicle for advancement.
- Arbitrage, supply-chain control, debt leverage, and political signaling are valid progression tools.
- The world rewards competence with access, authority, and monopoly rights; it punishes stagnation with confiscation, debt, and irrelevance.
- The protagonist's rise is gradual, contested, and economically strategic, not simply a martial ascent.
